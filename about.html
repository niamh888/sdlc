About This Course

This course is written and maintained by St John Lynch & Co Ltd. A consultancy that works in medical device software (SaMD, SiMD, Health Software and AI-enabled Health Software).

Technical qualifications

This course provides formal grounding in the standards this course teaches. The author is a member of the IEC 62304 committee and other standards necessary for medical device software development including AI-enabled Health Software.

Quality management

ISO 13485

Auditing and training in the quality management system standard IEC 62304 §4.1 requires every software lifecycle process to sit inside — the QMS this whole course assumes is already in place.

Information security

ISO 27001

Auditing and training in information security management — increasingly relevant to medical device software as connected devices and cloud-backed services widen the attack surface a manufacturer has to account for.

Health software security

IEC 81001-5-1

Auditing and training in health software security — the standard that intersects most directly with IEC 62304's own Amendment 1 cybersecurity additions covered throughout this course.

Experiential qualifications

Standards knowledge answers "what does the clause require?" Experience answers the harder question this course spends most of its time on: what does that actually look like in a real project?

Building, not just auditing

Software development for SaMD & health software

Hands-on development of Software as a Medical Device, AI-enabled devices, and health software more broadly — the practitioner's side of IEC 62304, not only the assessor's side.

To market, not just on paper

Regulatory strategy to market support

Experience carrying a regulatory strategy through to an actual market submission — the difference between knowing what a clause requires and knowing what a notified body or FDA reviewer actually pushes back on.

Auditing in practice

ISO & IEC audits, conducted

Direct audit experience against ISO 13485, ISO 27001 and IEC 81001-5-1 — seeing first-hand what evidence actually satisfies an auditor, and what looks complete on paper but does not.

The proof: this site practices what it teaches

This course's software development tool practices IEC 62304 development even when developing non-medical devices, because there is more learning in the doing. This ensures our staff have indepth understanding of IEC 62304 intricacies.

This training site's own software development is documented as if it were being developed as an IEC 62304 project with the same lifecycle discipline this course teaches, with the paperwork to show for it. See the project document register for the full set: a software development plan, a requirements specification, an architecture, a risk management file, a configuration management plan, and a problem resolution process, all real and current for this site, not invented as a demo.

A separate device example document set goes further: worked IEC 62304 documents — Standard Operating Procedures, in the shape a real QA/RA-controlled document takes — for a fictional infusion pump, precisely so you can see what the paperwork looks like when it is genuinely about patient risk, not just about a training website.
